🔒 Privacy Policy

✅ Your privacy is respected:
PeakDLE does not collect, store, or share ANY personal data directly.
⚠️ Exception: If you choose to donate via Buy Me a Coffee (third-party service), their own privacy policies apply.

1. Data Collection

The PeakDLE website is a completely free and anonymous daily guessing game. We do not collect any personal information about you.

More specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, first name, or email address
  • Your IP address
  • Your browsing data
  • Your scores or game attempts
  • Any information via cookies
  • Any data via forms (there are none)

Exception - Voluntary Donations: If you choose to support the project by making a donation via the Buy Me a Coffee button, you will be redirected to their external platform. In this case, Buy Me a Coffee will collect your payment data and personal information according to their own policies. We only receive notification of the donation and possibly your name/username if you choose to share it.

2. Cookies

The PeakDLE website does not use cookies directly, whether technical, analytical, or advertising.

Third-party Cookies - Buy Me a Coffee: If you visit the Buy Me a Coffee page via the donation button, their platform may place cookies on your device in accordance with their own cookie policy. These cookies are not under our control.

You can browse our main site with complete peace of mind without any cookies being placed by PeakDLE.

3. Local Storage

The site does not use local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) in your browser. No data is saved on your device.

This means your game sessions are not saved. Each visit is a new independent session.

4. Third-party Services and Tracking

The PeakDLE site does not use any third-party tracking or analytics services, including:

  • No Google Analytics
  • No Facebook Pixel
  • No advertising
  • No integrated social networks
  • No external comment system

All game data (items, images, descriptions) is stored locally on the server and loaded directly into your browser.

4.1 Donation Service - Buy Me a Coffee

The site offers an optional button to support the project via Buy Me a Coffee, a third-party donation collection service.

Important: When you click the donation button:

  • You are redirected to the external Buy Me a Coffee platform
  • Buy Me a Coffee collects your personal data (name, email, payment information)
  • Buy Me a Coffee uses its own cookies and tracking technologies
  • Their Privacy Policy and Terms of Use apply

What we receive: We only receive notification of the donation including the amount and possibly your name/username if you choose to share it. We never receive your payment information or banking details.

Voluntary nature: Using Buy Me a Coffee is entirely optional. The site remains 100% free and accessible without making a donation.

5. Hosting

The site is hosted by [HOST NAME]. According to standard web hosting practices, technical logs may be kept temporarily for server security and maintenance purposes (IP address, date/time of connection, pages visited).

These logs are automatically deleted after a short period and are never used for commercial or tracking purposes.

6. External Links

The site contains links to external sites, including:

  • The game Peak on Steam
  • The platform Buy Me a Coffee (if you use the donation button)

We are not responsible for the privacy policies of these third-party sites. We strongly encourage you to review their own privacy policies before interacting with them.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right of rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to portability
  • Right to object

However, since we do not collect any personal data, these rights do not apply in the context of using PeakDLE.
